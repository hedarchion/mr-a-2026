WHAT IS A ROUTINE?

Hobby vs routine — what is the difference?

🎨 Hobby

what you love to do
play football, read books
sometimes / on weekends

⏰ Daily routine

what you do every day
wake up, brush teeth, go to school
every morning / every day

A routine = every day. We use simple present for routines: I wake up at 6.30 a.m. every day.
SAY IT · SIMPLE PRESENT

We do it every day — so we say it like this

I / You / We / They
I wake up at 6.30 a.m.
They play football after school.
He / She
He wakes up at 6.30 a.m.
She plays football after school.

He/She → add -s

Routine = present simple. Not is waking. Just wake / wakes. ✓ I brush my teeth every morning.

Non-digital: chorus after teacher. Fingers: 1 = I wake, 2 = He wakes.

CHOOSE · SHOW 1-2-3 FINGERS

Which verb is correct?

She _____ her teeth every morning.
✓ A. brushes — She → add -es. brush → brushes. We add -es after -sh. B is for I/you. C needs is.

Listen for: She brush ✗ — needs -s. She is brush ✗ — no is.

WHERE + WHEN GOES AT THE END

Where do we put the time and place?

I have breakfast at home at 7 o'clock.

Place before time: at home → at 7 o'clock. Not in the middle of the verb.

✓ at the park in the evening
✗ at in the evening the park
✗ I at home have breakfast
Put details at the end: I go to school at 7 a.m. in the morning OR at the start: In the morning, I go to school. Never: I at 7 a.m. go school.

Teacher talk: WHO + VERB + WHERE/WHEN. Place before time.

AND vs BECAUSE

Add or give a reason?

I wake up early _____ I like morning assembly.
✓ B. because = reason. And just adds an idea: ...early and have breakfast. But = different/contrast.
Try: I wake up early and brush my teeth. (add) vs I wake up early because I want to be on time. (reason)

We move hobby → routine, but the guide words stay the same. Use at the front with a comma.

Front position is special in English — we put guide words first to organise the day.

THIS DAY IS MIXED UP

Which idea should be first?

Then, I have breakfast at home because I need energy.
Firstly, I wake up at 6.30 a.m. every day.
Finally, I go to school at 7.30 a.m. and feel ready.
Besides that, I brush my teeth at home at 6.45 a.m.
Firstly, I wake up… → Besides that, I brush… → Then, I have breakfast… → Finally, I go to school… (morning order = time order)

Tap Reveal. Ask: why can't Finally be first? Because it means last.

SPOT THE MISTAKE · CHOOSE THE FIX

Which sentence is correct?

✓ C — time at the end, and She → has. A puts time in the middle. B misses -s.
Typical errors to listen for today: I at 7am go school → move time to end. She have → She has.

# My Daily Routine — slide notes

## 1. welcome (prompt)

60-min Mon lesson, 31 Aug, 2:20-3:20. Retrieval bridges Week 30 hobby phrases into daily routines. Outcome (teacher-kept): describe a morning routine in four connected simple-present sentences.

## 2. recall-verbs (steps)

Retrieval of Week 30 verb phrases from 24/27 Aug decks. Reveal one step at a time; class choruses each phrase. Non-digital fallback: chorus.

## 3. recall-where-when (steps)

Retrieval of Week 30 place/time chunks. Same scaffold reused for routines today so the language load is use, not new vocabulary.

## 4. hobby-vs-routine (compare)

Bridge: hobby (sometimes) vs routine (every day). Expected: routine means every day, so we use simple present. Grammar book: present simple for regular-basis actions.

## 5. present-simple (compare)

Model: He/She adds -s (wakes, plays). Routine is present simple, never is + -ing. Listen for I am wake up.

## 6. she-brushes (cloze)

Click fills the blank inline; Reset clears it. B is for I/you. C needs is. Listen for She brush.

## 7. time-place (reveal)

Model: I have breakfast at home at 7 o'clock. Grammar book: phrasal time/place adverbials at end, place before time. Never in the middle of the verb.

## 8. mix-build (prompt)

Elicit 3 oral sentences from verb x where x when grid: wake up / have breakfast / go to school; at home / by bus; at 6.30 a.m. / 7 o'clock / 7.30 a.m. Formula: who + verb + where + when.

## 9. because-vs-and (cloze)

Click fills the blank inline; Reset clears it. Grammar book: because marks reason; and marks addition. Listen for and-chaining.

## 10. guide-words (steps)

Reuse of Week 30 linkers with fronted comma for time order. Reveal one step at a time; class choruses.

## 11. morning-order (order)

Items shown reversed; pupils click in sequence, click again to remove. Reveal restores time order. Ask why Finally cannot be first.

## 12. amira-model (writing)

Shared scaffold: Amira wakes up 6.30, brushes teeth at home 6.45, breakfast because she needs energy, school 7.30. Frame revealed on request. Watch She + -s throughout.

## 13. spot-correct (choice)

Repair check: A puts time in the middle; B misses -s. Selection records attempt; Reveal explains.

## 14. partner-talk (prompt)

2-min partner talk. A talks, B listens for -s and time at the end, then switch. Formula: present simple + time at the end + because if you can.

## 15. exit-ticket (prompt)

5-min individual exit, no model. Expect 3 linkers and 1 because/and; look for present simple, -s for he/she, time/place at end. Copying the class model without personalising is not mastery.

## 16. review (reveal)

Close: routine means every day, so present simple; because gives a reason. Forward bridge: add evening routine into one whole-day paragraph.

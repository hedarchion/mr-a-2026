## 1. retrieve-animals (steps)

Hands up / choral True-True-False, 6 min max. Elicit each verdict BEFORE tapping Next step. Say the why orally: terrapins are endangered; forests gone means homes gone; eco means safe. Misconception: eco-friendly misread as harmful (cf. Module p.35 Q4 False). Follow-up: ask what poaching means (one word: illegal hunting). Bridge: same skill today — match the right sentence. Retrieval from 2026-09-14 deck only; do not reteach p.34.

## 2. words-hard-1 (steps)

Teacher models each once, pupils repeat twice; drill the stressed caps syllable. Both words verified non-A2 (0 matches, A2 Key Aug 2025). Keep Malay brief; examples are paraphrases, not book verbatim.

## 3. words-hard-2 (steps)

## 6. match-bulb-1 (matrix)

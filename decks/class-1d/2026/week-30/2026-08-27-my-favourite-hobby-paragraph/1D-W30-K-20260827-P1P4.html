A Hobby Club Afternoon

Every Thursday after school, the Hobby Club meets in the school library. Pupils can try different hobbies there. Some pupils read books in the reading corner. Others listen to music with headphones or make small things at the craft table.

Last Thursday, Nisha went to the club with her friend, Samir. Nisha enjoys making small things, so she chose the craft table. First, she made a paper flower. Then, she used a red pen to write her name on it. Samir likes reading, but he did not choose the reading corner at first. He wanted to listen to a new song. After ten minutes, he went to the reading corner and read a comic book. Samir sometimes brings his sister, Aina, to the club, but she was at home that day.

At four o'clock, the teacher asked the pupils to share their work. Nisha showed her paper flower to the group. She said that making things helps her feel calm. Samir told the class about his comic book. Both pupils said they wanted to return next Thursday. Nisha likes the reading corner too because it is quiet. Before they left, the teacher gave every pupil a small blue notebook for hobby ideas.

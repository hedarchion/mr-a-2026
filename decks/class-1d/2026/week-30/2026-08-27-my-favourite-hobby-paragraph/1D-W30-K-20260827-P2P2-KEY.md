# 1D-W30-K-20260827-P2P2 — Teacher Key and Rubric

**Form 1 English, Paper 2 Part 2: Notes Expansion** · **Topic:** My Favourite Hobby · **Total:** 20 marks

## Model answer (94 words)

My favourite hobby is playing badminton. Firstly, I play badminton at the court near my house every Saturday morning. I usually play with my sister because she is good at the game. Besides that, we practise for one hour and help each other to improve. Then, we drink water and talk about our day. I like this hobby because it is fun and it keeps me healthy. Finally, I feel tired but happy after playing badminton. I want to play more often during the school holidays.

The response develops all three notes, uses a clear beginning-to-end sequence and shows a simple conclusion. Relevant personal details may differ.

## Feedback priority

First check that pupils have written complete sentences and developed every note. Then give one next step: add a useful detail, use a linker, or check a key verb/word order.

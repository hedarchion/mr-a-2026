PLAN YOUR 4 SENTENCES

What can I write about?

1. What is your hobby?
2. Where / when / with who?
3. Why do you like it?
4. How do you feel?
My favourite hobby is playing football. / I play it at the field with my friends every evening. / I like it because it is exciting. / I always feel happy when I play football.
HELP THE READER FOLLOW YOU

Words that guide the reader

Firstly,
first idea
Besides that,
add an idea
Then,
next idea
Finally,
last idea
Firstly, my favourite hobby is playing badminton.
Besides that, I usually play it with my sister.
Then, we practise together for one hour.
Finally, I feel happy after playing.

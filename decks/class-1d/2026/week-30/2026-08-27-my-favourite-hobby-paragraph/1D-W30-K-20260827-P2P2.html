In your class, you have been talking about hobbies.

In about 90 words, write an essay about your favourite hobby. You may use the notes below to help you.

MY FAVOURITE HOBBY• What is your favourite hobby?
• Where, when and with whom do you do it?
• Why do you like it and how do you feel afterwards?

Write your essay.

1. Plan your ideas

My hobby:
Where / when / with whom:
Why / feeling:

2. Helpful language

Hobby verbs: play, read, listen to, ride
Details: at home, at the park, after school, with my friends
Reasons: fun, exciting, relaxing, keeps me healthy
Linkers: Firstly, Besides that, Then, Finally, because, but

3. Build complete sentences

Firstly, my favourite hobby is …
Besides that, I usually …
Then, I …
Finally, I feel … because …

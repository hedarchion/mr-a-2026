# 1E-W31-J-20260911-GRAMMAR-MIXED — KEY (Mixed revision)

Form 1 English · Grammar practice · 10 marks, one mark per item, dichotomous scoring.

## Section A — quantifiers

1. Many — visitors are plural countable; two hundred is large. (Much + countable fails.)
2. a few — five storybooks: small countable number. (a little needs an uncountable noun.)
3. much — noise is uncountable.
4. some — sugar is uncountable and the amount is unknown. (many + uncountable fails.)
5. a lot of — ninety stamps: large countable number. (much + countable fails.)

## Section B — describing people

6. She is a tall girl. — the adjective comes before the noun.
7. They are strong players. — the adjective comes before the noun.
8. tall and friendly — tallly is not a word; adjectives describe people, not -ly adverbs here.
9. He is — singular subject takes is.
10. Any two suitable adjectives (e.g. tall, kind, brave, helpful, cheerful). Accept any two real adjectives that can describe a person; reject adverbs, nouns, or verbs.

## Administration

Brief, individual, formative, ungraded, teacher-marked, ordinary classroom supports. Item 10: one mark only if both slots hold suitable adjectives. Limitation: unpiloted classroom instrument; treat scores as practice evidence only.

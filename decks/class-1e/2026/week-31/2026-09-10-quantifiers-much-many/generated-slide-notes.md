# Quantifiers · much, many and friends — slide notes

## 1. notice-pair (prompt)

60-min Khamis lesson, Module p86. Deductive opening: pupils notice the contrast before any rule. Collect oral guesses, give nothing away.

## 2. count-sort (sort)

Prerequisite check: countable/uncountable decides every quantifier today. Teacher clicks the category the class proposes. Grammar book: countable unit nouns vs mass nouns.

## 3. many-model (reveal)

First pattern from pupil deduction. Elicit the rule before revealing. Grammar book Much-many: many with countable plurals.

## 4. much-model (reveal)

Second pattern. Stress negatives/questions at elementary level per grammar book. Drill chorally.

## 5. some-few-lot (reveal)

Third pattern, Module p86 points 2-5. A lot of works for both kinds in affirmatives. Keep brisk; the 10 questions do the heavy lifting.

60-min Rabu lesson. Activate spending schema before Raju's email. Oral pair answers, 3 min. Bridge from 2 Sep describing people: today we help a person, not describe one.

## 2. raju-email (passage)

Paraphrased Module p85 stimulus, kept short for projection. Expected: advice on managing spending. Teacher plays the NotebookLM primer video before or after this slide per SOT plan.

## 3. reply-job (choice)

## 4. email-shape (reveal)

Model the Module suggested answer shape without copying its sentences. Elicit parts before revealing.

## 5. advice-words (reveal)

Core advice frames for the book task. Drill should + verb chorally. Misconception to listen for: should to buy / should not saving (wrong verb form).

## 6. kinder-advice (cloze)

## 7. stronger-tip (choice)

Organisation dimension: connect tip to reason. Ask a volunteer to add a different because ending.

## 8. email-order (order)

Click sentences in sequence. Reveal restores canonical order. Names the dependency: greeting opens, closing ends.

## 9. module-task (writing)

Module p85 book task, individual, ~15 min. Frame hidden until Reveal; weaker pupils may keep it. Collect books for marks per SOT.

## 10. exit-tip (prompt)

Exit evidence, 3 min oral. Listen for should + verb and one reason. Weak or missing reasons become Thursday's retrieval bridge.

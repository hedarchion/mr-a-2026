# Poetry Is Easier Than You Think — 1E — slide notes

## 1. 1e-01-hook (choice)

Sayable: Could this be a poem? Which one is BEST? Silent 10s, gesture vote (fingers 1/2/3), take 3 hands max: What makes you think so? Expect split vote, no-rhyme dominant. OPEN VOTE: renderer needs one canonical; Not sure is a neutral placeholder only — accept all answers, log rhyme-equals-rule misconception, do not correct yet. Bridge: Let's meet a real poet. 1E: accept one-word answers. [ELICIT ~3 min]

## 2. 1e-02-swing (choice)

Sayable: Read the two lines. Which one is BEST? Read excerpt aloud twice; pupils repeat second line chorally before voting. Expect near-unanimous B; if journey/dream, note over-inference. Follow-up oral (no writing): Could you write about something ordinary — football, rain, your cat? Collect 4–5 ideas. Bridge: If a swing can be a poem, what about rhyme? [NOTICE ~4 min]

## 3. 1e-03-rhyme (choice)

Sayable: Say the last word of each line. Which one is BEST? A/B fingers. Expect fast No; if Yes, say pair aloud once, no phonics lecture. Log rhyme-as-rule. Bridge: If rhyme is not the secret, what makes these lines interesting? [COMPARE ~3 min]

## 4. 1e-04-fog (passage)

Sayable: What picture do you see in your head? Read aloud once normal, once slowly; point to each line; pupils repeat sits / looks / moves chorally. Accept any visual (cat, smoke, ghost animal). Do NOT define personification yet. Word bank voiceable if stuck: fog, cat feet, sits, looks, moves. Bridge: Fog cannot really sit. So what did the poet do? [NOTICE ~4 min]

## 5. 1e-05-person (choice)

Sayable: Can fog really have feet? Which one is BEST? After vote show micro-examples orally: The wind whispers. The moon watches. The rain dances. Term pupil wording: personification gives something non-human a human or living action. Try-it oral with frame + bank (sleeps / waits / smiles): The door ___ . The sun ___ . Accept any living-action verb. Misconception: poem must be literally true. Bridge: Poets also choose small exact words. [EXPLAIN ~4 min]

## 6. 1e-06-specific (choice)

Sayable: Which one is BEST — A or B? Key line: Specific words make pictures. Counter long-word myth: Did B use difficult English? No — exact English. Try-it oral with frame: The dog ___ . (sleeps / waits / barks / watches). Accept any specific verb. Bridge: Now watch one plain sentence become a poem. [COMPARE ~4 min]

## 7. 1e-07-bell (steps)

Sayable per step: What can we add? What stronger verb? Where does it shout? Build orally BEFORE revealing each line; offer verb choices (rings / shouts / sings) rather than open elicitation. Accept variants (cries, hot evening). Say: We did not need difficult English. Summary: detail, strong verb, image. Bridge: Those moves have names. [MODEL ~4 min]

## 8. 1e-08-toolbox (passage)

SYNTHESIS REFERENCE (~3 min, no new teaching, nothing hidden). All five visible from the start. Point to each; pupils repeat each example chorally. Point back: 1 = rain, 2-4 just now, 5 = bell break. Sayable: Poetry is making choices with ordinary words. Bridge: Now test yourself — which tool is this?

## 9. 1e-09a-moon (choice)

Sayable: Which one is BEST? Whole-class fingers 1/2/3 before reveal. Contrast: not simile — no like/as. [PRACTISE]

## 10. 1e-09b-wind (choice)

Sayable: Which one is BEST? Counter wind-confusion: Who runs? The brother — a person. The tool here is like. Not personification. [PRACTISE]

## 11. 1e-09c-run (choice)

Sayable: Which one is BEST? Contrast: no comparison, no non-human action. Keep same 3 choices for predictability. Total Beat 9 ~4 min. Bridge: Now use real noticing. [PRACTISE]

## 12. 1e-10-camera (prompt)

Sayable: Do not invent a new world. Tell me what is really here. Build bank publicly from pupil words (6–8: wet shoes, grey sky, noisy rain, cold floor, umbrellas, dripping bags, sleepy students, water on window). Accept feelings — sleepy, lonely — especially 1E emotion bridge (affirm feeling-subjects miss/love). Volume of concrete nouns/verbs matters, not poetic quality. Keep bank visible into next beat. Bridge: Now use these words. [ELICIT ~5 min]

## 13. 1e-11-together (steps)

Sayable staged: What does the rain do? (verb: taps / knocks / sings, 1E choose) Where? What does it sound like? (It sounds like ___. Tiny fingers? Small drums?) If rain were a person — waits? listens? watches? Title decided aloud: Rain (one word). Load-bearing line verbatim: This is not a perfect poem. It is a poem because we made choices — specific verb, simile, human action, line breaks. Accept variant verbs. Leave visible into next beat as confidence anchor; remove before Beat 15 so nobody copies. [MODEL ~5 min]

## 14. 1e-12-choose (prompt)

Sayable: You only need ONE subject. Point to it now. Visible commitment in 60 seconds (finger on choice / whisper to neighbour). Steer strugglers to RAIN / SCHOOL / MOON (banked language exists). 1E emotion bridge: pupils choosing SOMETHING I MISS / LOVE — affirm feeling-subjects as valid poem material. Bridge: Here is a recipe if you need steps. [COMMIT ~2 min]

## 15. 1e-13-recipe (passage)

Sayable: A simple recipe — training wheels, not rules. Read once aloud; point to toolbox links (line 5 = simile, line 6 = personification, line 7 = repetition). Reassure: You do not have to use every line exactly. Do NOT collect writing yet. 2E preview not needed for 1E. Single-slide recipe kept after fit check at 1280x665; split only if browser verify overflows. Bridge: Plan in 30 seconds, then write. [REFERENCE ~3 min]

## 16. 1e-14-plan (prompt)

Sayable: Copy the frames. Use your own words. Thirty seconds. Model with rain in 60s max, then pupils fill silently. Circulate: first check = subject + one notice. Hard stop at 2 min. Frames: My subject / 3 notices / verb / comparison (It is like ___) / human action (It ___). Bridge: Now write. Six to eight lines. [PLAN ~2 min]

## 17. 1e-15-write (writing)

Sayable: Start now. One subject. Your choices. 8-min hard stop (timer). Circulate priority: one clear subject → lines appearing → one concrete detail → one tool. Rescue ladder voiceable: What can you see? What does it do? What does it sound like? What is it like? If it were a person, what would it do? Non-starters: point to Beat 14 frames, dictate starter (My ___ ...). Remove Beat 11 model from view. 1E early-finish extension only: Repeat your best line once more. Do NOT heavily correct grammar. Evidence: titled 6–8 line poem. [APPLY ~8 min]

## 18. 1e-16-check (passage)

Sayable: Do not ask, Is it perfect? Ask, Which line can I make better? 3 min: 1 min check, 2 min rewrite one line. Affirm revisions adding specificity/tool even if grammar imperfect. Descriptive improvement language only, never public marks. Bridge: So what IS poetry, after doing it? [REVISE ~3 min]

## 19. 1e-17-what (choice)

Sayable: Think about your poem. Which one is BEST? Expect C; if A/B replay evidence (comes/feet did not rhyme; cold/taps were not hard). Follow-up oral: What is one choice you made? (simile / strong verb / repeated word / line break) — take 3 answers. Bridge: One last challenge — prove it in two lines. [CONSOLIDATE ~2 min]

## 20. 1e-18-exit (prompt)

Sayable: Write two lines now. Use one choice from your toolbox. Take 3–4 shares; for each ask class: What choice did the writer make? Reward any real choice — human action, comparison, strong break — not one perfect answer. Possibles: The moon / watches me. OR The bright moon hangs / like a lamp in the sky. Do NOT judge beautiful. Final line: You have written a poem. Collect exit lines + Beat 15 poems as evidence. [TRANSFER ~2 min]

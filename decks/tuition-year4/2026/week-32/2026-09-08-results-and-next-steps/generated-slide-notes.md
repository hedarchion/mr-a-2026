# Year 4 Tuition · Results and Next Steps — slide notes

## 1. welcome-back (prompt)

Welcome-back framing, ~3 min. This check is a starting point, not a grade. Pre-empt check-equals-failure thinking; if she names spelling or small words as the grow-item, confirm that is the Session 2 job.

## 2. strengths-next (compare)

Strengths vs next steps, ~4 min. She points to one grow-item; reframe grow-next as small fixes, not failure. Example words only.

## 3. fix-with (reveal)

With model, ~5 min. She tries aloud first. Session 1 error was from my mother; cue together-or-where. One follow-up swap (with my father), then stop.

## 4. fix-his (reveal)

His model, ~5 min. Session 1 error was him bag; cue whether him can come before bag. Contrast her bag once if time allows.

## 5. spell-bakery (reveal)

Bakery spelling, ~5 min. She says and spells first; teacher never shows the spelling before her attempt. People-or-shop cue for the bakers error; air-write -ery once more.

## 6. learn-clinic (reveal)

Clinic as a new word, ~4 min. Unknown is expected; allow wait time. Accept hospital as near, then contrast that a clinic is small. Use it once in a sentence.

## 7. session-2-plan (prompt)

Session 2 hook, ~3 min. She names one target; reassure little steps, not everything at once. Full repair lands in Session 2: short games with the four words.

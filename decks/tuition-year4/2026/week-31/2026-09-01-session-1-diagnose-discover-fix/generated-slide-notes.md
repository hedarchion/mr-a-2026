# Year 4 Tuition · Session 1 · Diagnose, Discover, Fix — slide notes

## 1. welcome (prompt)

Session 1 opening, ~1 min plus 4 min oral chat (favourite subject, yesterday, best friend, after school). Frame as a starting check, not a graded test. Observe comprehension, response length, tense choice, confidence; do not correct yet. Visual: Lucide clipboard-check, decorative, initial only.

## 2. q-1 (choice)

Reading — locate place in a short notice. Repeat the direction if needed; do not explain. Learner answers on paper; teacher records the spoken choice neutrally.

## 3. q-2 (choice)

Reading — retrieve an instruction. Misconception: picks everyday school shoes from habit instead of the note.

## 4. q-3 (choice)

Reading — interpret a prohibition. Wording fixed per validation: names the referent of Neither and removes the elliptical bare option. Probe if wrong: which word proves drinks are allowed?

## 5. q-4 (choice)

Reading — match a functional response. Kept as choice per storyboard: producing the reply unaided would confound speaking confidence with function matching.

## 6. q-5 (choice)

Language — past-time verb in a continuous cloze. Same garden story repeats on Q5–Q8 with numbered blanks; other gaps stay blank so no answer leaks early.

## 7. q-6 (choice)

Language — preposition and collocation in a continuous cloze.

## 8. q-7 (choice)

Language — possessive determiner with a clear referent. him and he are ungrammatical before the noun.

## 9. q-8 (choice)

Language — past-time verb in a continuous cloze. Bridge to the Gardening Day notice next.

## 10. q-9 (short)

Reading — retrieve the date. Stem fixed per validation (What is the date, not When is it) so Saturday cannot satisfy it. Typable: teacher types neutrally; variants judged at review, never auto-graded.

## 11. q-10 (short)

Reading — distinguish nearby location details. Accept the fuller phrase; reject in the library alone. Probe: which words prove the exact place?

## 12. q-11 (short)

Reading — retrieve the required object. Misconception: answers flowers, borrowing the activity word instead of the object.

## 13. q-12 (short)

Reading — retrieve the planned activity. A single noun (flowers) without the verb signals a phrase-versus-sentence gap; log for the sentence-repair path.

## 14. q-13 (choice)

Reading — match a preference across texts. Club cards repeat on Q13–Q15, trimmed to the decision-relevant lines to hold the projection word budget; full legacy wording is in the teacher key below.

## 15. q-14 (choice)

Reading — match a preference across texts.

## 16. q-15 (choice)

Reading — retrieve a requirement across texts. Stem fixed per validation (Which club, not Who) to match the A/B/C response mode.

## 17. q-16 (short)

Vocabulary — retrieve and spell a community-place word. Marking is case-insensitive; spelling must be exact. Separate retrieval (knows the place) from spelling (writes it) at conference.

## 18. q-17 (short)

Vocabulary — retrieve and spell a community-place word.

## 19. q-18 (short)

Vocabulary — retrieve and spell a community-place word. Hospital is a sensible meaning substitution but fails this spelling task; record it as meaning-ok, spelling-miss.

## 20. response-review (review)

Single review slide for the 18 diagnostic items only. Check every captured choice and typed response with the learner; fix teacher typos (not learner errors) directly in the grid, then Export JSON. Writings A and B are paper-only and excluded from the export.

## 21. writing-a (writing)

Writing A: Part 6 short message, post-diagnostic and unscored. Expect three content points plus greeting and closing. Do not intervene on missed jobs now; count them at conference. Collect the unaided baseline FIRST (5 min, papers down); click Hint only afterwards for the repair pass. Expected misuse: copies both can-or-cannot without deleting one.

## 22. writing-b (writing)

Writing B: abbreviated Part 7 notes-expansion, post-diagnostic and unscored. Collect the unaided baseline FIRST (5 min, papers down); click Hint only afterwards for the repair pass. running race = the competition in the notes (confirm orally). Expected misuse: It is water / It is running race (noun for feeling word) — offer fun or exciting.

## 23. conference (prompt)

Diagnostic conference, ~8 min. Pick three representative errors spanning causes: knowledge (did not know the form), reading (missed the clue), vocabulary (one word blocked meaning), checking (knew it on re-read). Compare baseline and writings on task fulfilment, complete sentences, meaning, spelling and punctuation, and connected ideas. Confirm Strong, Developing or Weak only after the learner explains.

## 24. repair-model (compare)

One-bottleneck repair, ~7 min total with the repair attempt. Default sentence path shown; the teacher chooses one path from the conference evidence instead: missed jobs (count each job to its sentence), blocked word (read before and after it), carelessness (STOP, READ, PROVE), strong baseline (add precise detail). Elicit the learner's decisions; the teacher may scribe but must not author.

## 25. exit-challenge (writing)

Fresh exit challenge, ~5 min. Expect 3–4 complete, understandable sentences showing the repair. Compare with the baseline on the same five dimensions.

## 26. reflection (prompt)

Close, ~2 min. The learner names one concrete change. Record the next priority privately for Sessions 2–4. Confirm with the teacher which 2026 school instrument the learner will sit before any format-specific practice.

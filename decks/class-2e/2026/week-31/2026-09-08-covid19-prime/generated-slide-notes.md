# Small Words, Strong Sentences — slide notes

## 1. hook (prompt)

60-min Tue lesson, sl_t1. Opening hook for Module p.69. Monday one-liner only, no reteaching. Outcomes (teacher-kept): read the passage clearly for PBD; use 6 new words in own sentences.

## 2. prime-changes (steps)

Prime the passage ideas in simple vocabulary. Partner talk 1 min: which change still helps us today? Point and say why.

## 3. connected-home (image)

PURPOSEFUL VISUAL (~2 min). Reused local family scene as a concrete bridge to cooperation and staying connected at home. It supports discussion but does not reveal any target-word answer.

## 4. word-resilient (reveal)

Elicit first, then reveal. Word 1 of 6.

## 5. word-severity (reveal)

Word 2 of 6. Sets up the weak-vs-strong slide.

## 6. word-droplet (reveal)

Word 3 of 6. -let spotlight per SOT: drop + -let, book + -let, leaf + -let.

## 7. word-vital (reveal)

Word 4 of 6.

## 8. word-disruption (reveal)

Word 5 of 6.

## 9. word-significant (reveal)

Word 6 of 6. Module gloss follows p.69. Misconception: pupils copy definitions — enforce show, don't repeat.

## 10. all-words-mix (wordmix)

Required all-words synthesis. Keep all six word–meaning pairs visible. Select any word and idea, then invite pupils to accept, improve or reject the combination with a reason. Some combinations need imaginative context; that judgement is part of the mix-and-match task.

## 11. weak-strong (compare)

Frame: word + action + detail + because/so + result. Explain that A gives a definition but does not show the word in use. Copy the habit, not the sentence.

## 12. pbd-routine (steps)

Teacher goes around testing against LS 3.1.5 (read the article) and LS 3.1.4 (word meanings); about 8 readers. Passage stays in the book (copyright). No names in artifacts.

## 13. exit-sentence (writing)

5 min silent exit. Bridge: Thursday turns the p.70 handwashing essay into an information-transfer table. Collect short/long books after.

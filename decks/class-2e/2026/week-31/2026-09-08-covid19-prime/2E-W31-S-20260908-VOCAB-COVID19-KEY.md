# KEY — 2E-W31-S-20260908-VOCAB-COVID19 (Strong Sentences)

Formative, teacher-marked, ordinary supports. 12 marks total: 2 per item (1 = correct word use showing meaning in a new idea, 1 = complete, sensible sentence with capital + full stop).

Reject: copying the given meaning verbatim, single-word answers, or sentences that do not show the meaning.

## Acceptable model sentences (accept any suitable sentence showing the meaning)

1. **resilient** — Our class became resilient when we kept studying online during the flood.
2. **severity** — Wearing masks reduced the severity of the flu so fewer pupils missed school.
3. **droplet** — The virus spreads through tiny droplets when people cough without covering their mouths.
4. **vital** — Handwashing played a vital role in our school because it stopped germs spreading.
5. **disruption** — The lockdown caused disruption to football training for two months.
6. **significant** — Learning to use video calls was significant because we could still meet our teachers.

## -let note (slide 3)

- droplet = drop + -let (small). Parallels: booklet (small book), leaflet (small leaf). Check pupils do not write "droplettes" or use "droplet" as a verb.

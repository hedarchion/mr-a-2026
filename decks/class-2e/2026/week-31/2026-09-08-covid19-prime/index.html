VOCAB · TAP TO CHECK

Six words. Tap a word, say the meaning, then check.

-let spotlight: drop + -let = droplet (tiny drop). Same pattern: book → booklet, leaf → leaflet.

MODEL · COPY THE HABIT, NOT THE SENTENCE

Model, then you build.

Weak (copies the meaning): “Severity means serious.”
Strong (uses it): “Vaccines reduced the severity of illness so fewer people became very sick.”
Frame: word + action + detail + because / so + result. Your sentence must show the meaning — not repeat the definition.

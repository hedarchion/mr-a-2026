How Covid-19 Changed the World — Strong Sentences [12 marks: 2 per sentence]

Name: Class: 2E Date:

Reading: Form 2 Module p.69. Task: Write ONE new sentence for each word. Show the meaning in a new idea — do not copy the meaning. Use because / so / when in at least three sentences.

1. resilient — strong again after difficulty
Sentence frame: _____ became resilient when/so _____ .
2. severity — how serious an illness is
Sentence frame: _____ reduced the severity of _____ so _____ .
3. droplet (drop + -let = tiny) — a very tiny drop
Sentence frame: The virus spreads through tiny droplets when _____ .
4. vital — very important
Sentence frame: _____ played a vital role in _____ because _____ .
5. disruption — stopping normal life
Sentence frame: The _____ caused disruption to _____ .
6. significant — very great / very large
Sentence frame: _____ was significant because _____ .

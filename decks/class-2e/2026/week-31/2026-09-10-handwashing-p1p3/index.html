READ · MODULE P.70 ESSAY AS PASSAGE

How to Wash Your Hands Properly

Washing our hands properly is one of the best ways to stay healthy. It helps prevent the spread of germs. To wash correctly, follow some simple steps. First, wet your hands with clean, running water. Next, apply enough soap to cover all hand surfaces. Then, rub your hands together for at least 20 seconds. Wash the backs of your hands, between your fingers and under your nails. Finally, rinse well under clean water and dry with a tissue or an air dryer. By washing properly, we can protect ourselves and others.
Source: Form 2 Module, p.70 suggested answer (shortened, teacher-requested excerpt).

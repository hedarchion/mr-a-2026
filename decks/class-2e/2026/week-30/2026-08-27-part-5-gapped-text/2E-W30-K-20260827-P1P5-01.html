The Festival Film

For the town youth festival, Hana and her friends decided to make a short film about local food stalls. They wanted visitors to discover small family businesses that were often missed by tourists. Hana's teacher reminded them to be polite because the stall owners would be busy serving customers. [35] On Saturday morning, they met at the market with a camera, a notebook and a small microphone.

However, Hana realised that the camera battery was nearly empty. The group could not begin the interviews until the camera had enough power. [36] While they waited, her friends prepared the first questions and checked the market map.

Soon, one stall owner began making noodles. Steam rose from the hot pan while customers watched from nearby tables. [37] The owner smiled and continued working as the group recorded the scene.

A loud bus passed just as the owner lifted the noodles. Its engine made it difficult to hear the owner's explanation. [38] The second recording was clear, so they thanked the owner and moved to another stall.

By noon, they had enough scenes, but they still needed an ending. Hana wanted the film to finish with a friendly message for visitors. [39] Everyone agreed, and they filmed the final scene near the market entrance.

That evening, the group watched every clip at Hana's house. They chose the clearest scenes and put them in the right order. [40] The finished film was shown at the festival the following weekend.
G. Before filming, they wrote a simple plan and asked the stall owners for permission.
D. She borrowed a power bank from her older brother and charged the camera while her friends prepared the first scene.
A. The group filmed quietly from the side so customers could continue shopping.
H. Because of the noise, they recorded that part again after the bus had gone.
C. Hana suggested showing the stall owner smiling and thanking the visitors.
B. That evening, they edited the clips and added captions explaining each dish.
E. The camera had been used by the festival organisers for a different event the previous week.
F. Hana had already added captions before the group began filming.

A Small School Garden

Last term, the Eco Club at Green Valley School decided to create a small herb garden behind the science block. The space had been empty for years, so the students wanted to make it useful for lessons and for visitors. Their science teacher said that plants would grow best if the club planned carefully from the beginning. [35] This helped them decide where each plant should go and which areas needed more water.

The students soon found a problem when they began preparing the ground. It had not been used for gardening, and rubbish had collected there over time. [36] Therefore, they mixed compost into it and removed the stones. After a week, they planted mint, basil and chilli.

Most of the plants grew well, but the mint began spreading across the nearby beds. It reached towards the basil before the students noticed the problem. [37] The club moved some mint into pots so that the other plants had enough space.

Watering was another challenge during the school holidays. The students usually took turns after classes, but the school would be closed for a fortnight. [38] To solve this problem, the students asked the security guard to check the garden and water it every morning.

When school reopened, the garden looked healthy. The herbs had survived the holiday, and several plants had grown taller. [39] They picked some herbs and used them in a simple salad during a science lesson.

The project also taught the students an important lesson. The club understood why one quick afternoon of planting was not enough. [40] Now, the Eco Club plans to add a small sign explaining how visitors can care for the plants.
F. Before buying seeds, club members measured the sunlight and drew a simple plan.
A. The soil was dry and full of small stones.
H. It was growing faster than the other herbs.
C. No club members would be at school for two weeks.
G. The students were delighted to see new leaves and bright flowers.
D. They learned that careful planning and regular care were as important as planting seeds.
B. Several students suggested painting the garden fence bright green.
E. The science teacher gave the club a list of useful herbs.

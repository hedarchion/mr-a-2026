# Poetry Is Easier Than You Think — 2E — slide notes

## 1. 2e-b01-hook (choice)

Beat 1 hook/diagnostic, 3 min. Sayable: Could this be a poem? Take 3 hands max: What makes you think so? Expect split vote, no-rhyme dominant. Log rhyme-as-rule misconception, do not correct yet. Bridge: Let's meet a real poet.

## 2. 2e-b02-swing (choice)

Beat 2 authentic model, 4 min. Read excerpt aloud twice. Expect near-unanimous B; D signals over-inference. Reveal, then elicit orally: Could you write about something ordinary — football, rain, your cat? Collect 4-5 ideas, no writing. Bridge: If a swing can be a poem, what about rhyme?

## 3. 2e-b03-rhyme (choice)

Beat 3 misconception correction, 3 min. Have class say the pair aloud once if A appears. Log rhyme-as-rule. Bridge: If rhyme is not the secret, what makes these lines interesting? No cat picture — protects Beats 4-5 discovery.

## 4. 2e-b04-fog (passage)

Beat 4 full-Fog camera, 4 min. Read aloud once at normal speed, once slowly. Accept any reasonable visual (cat, smoke, quiet animal sitting). Do NOT define personification yet. Reveal line (spoken): He does not explain fog. He makes us imagine it. Bridge: Fog cannot really sit. So what did the poet do?

## 5. 2e-b05-fog-feet (choice)

Beat 5 personification discovered, 4 min. 2E: elicit without verb bank first; frames available if stuck. After reveal show micro-examples orally: The wind whispers. The moon watches. The rain dances. Try-it orally: The door ___. The sun ___. Accept any living-action verb. Follow-up: Is the moon really watching? Bridge: Which words make pictures?

## 6. 2e-b06-specific (choice)

Beat 6 specific words, 4 min. Key line: Specific words make pictures. Counter long-word myth: Did B use difficult English? No — exact English. Try-it: The dog is nice. Change ONE word. Accept any specific verb. Bridge: Now watch one plain sentence become a poem.

## 7. 2e-b07-bell-build (steps)

Beat 7 model sentence-to-poem, 4 min. Build orally BEFORE revealing each line. Sayables: What can we add? What stronger verb? Where does it shout? Accept variants (cries, hot evening). Say: We did not need difficult English. Final two-line break pre-teaches line breaks. Bridge: Those moves have names. Here are all five together.

## 8. 2e-b08-toolbox (passage)

Beat 8 REQUIRED all-concepts synthesis, 4 min. All five visible from initial; nothing hidden, no new teaching. Say: These are your five choices. You only need one in your poem. Pupils repeat each example chorally. Point back: 1 in rain, 2-4 just now, 5 in the bell. Result line (spoken): Poetry is making choices with ordinary words. Bridge: Now test yourself — which tool is this?

## 9. 2e-b09a-moon (choice)

Beat 9A rapid classification. Fingers 1/2/3 vote before reveal. 2E: ask why not the other two? A is not simile — no like/as. Contrast distractors.

## 10. 2e-b09b-brother (choice)

Beat 9B. Expect confusion with personification (wind is non-human). Counter: Who runs? The brother — a person. The tool here is the comparison word like.

## 11. 2e-b09c-run (choice)

Beat 9C. C is neither comparison nor non-human action — no like/as, no living action given. Pacing: 4 min total for all three 9A/B/C.

## 12. 2e-b10-camera (prompt)

Beat 10 guided idea generation, 5 min. Say: Do not invent a new world. Tell me what is really here. Build word bank publicly from pupil words (take 6-8: wet shoes, grey sky, noisy rain, cold floor, umbrellas, dripping bags, sleepy students, water on window). Accept feelings as valid material. Keep bank visible into Beat 11. Bridge: Now use these words. Let's write one together.

## 13. 2e-b11-rain-together (steps)

Beat 11 guided construction, 5 min. Staged sayables: What does the rain do? Where? What does it sound like? Can we give it a human action? Verb choices if stuck: taps, knocks, sings. Emphasise verbatim: This is not a perfect poem. It is a poem because we made choices. Title decided explicitly. Leave visible into Beat 12; remove before Beat 15 so pupils cannot copy.

## 14. 2e-b12-choice (prompt)

Beat 12 choice/anxiety reduction, 2 min. Get visible commitment in 60 seconds (finger on choice, whisper to neighbour). Open choice, no key. Spoken line: One clear subject makes a stronger poem. Bridge: Here is a recipe if you need steps.

## 15. 2e-b13a-recipe-1 (steps)

Beat 13 recipe part 1 of 2 (same wording halves; split only to fit limits), 3 min total. Read aloud once. Link to toolbox: line 5 uses simile, line 6 personification, line 7 repetition. Spoken: Skip a line if you need to. Keep 6 to 8 lines. 2E preview: strong writers, line 8 changes the feeling.

## 16. 2e-b13b-recipe-2 (steps)

Beat 13 recipe part 2 of 2. Same beat, identical wording halves. Training wheels, not rules. Bridge: Plan in 30 seconds, then write.

## 17. 2e-b14-plan (prompt)

Beat 14 model planning, 2 min. Model rain in 60 sec, then pupils fill silently. Say: Copy the frames. Use your own words. Thirty seconds. Rescue: Stuck? Steal from our word bank — cold, window, grey — then change one word. Circulate for blanks: first check subject + one notice. Spoken close: Notes first, lines second. Now turn notes into lines.

## 18. 2e-b15-write (writing)

Beat 15 independent transfer, 8 min hard stop (timer). Conditions: 6-8 lines, title, specific details, at least ONE of simile/personification/repetition/line-breaks, no rhyme needed. 2E extension (slide hint + spoken after 5 lines): Strong writers: add a second picture or change the feeling in the last two lines. Rescue ladder spoken: What can you see? What does it do? What does it sound like? What is it like? If it were a person, what would it do? Circulate priority: one subject, lines appearing, one detail, one tool. Non-starters: point to Beat 14 frames, dictate starter My ___. Remove Beat 11 model from view. Do NOT heavily correct grammar.

## 19. 2e-b16-check (prompt)

Beat 16 self-check/revise, 3 min: 1 min check, 2 min rewrite one line. Say: Do not ask, Is it perfect? Ask, Which line can I make better? Affirm revisions adding specificity or a tool even if grammar imperfect. Spoken close: Poets revise choices; one stronger line is enough today. Bridge: So what IS poetry, after doing it?

## 20. 2e-b17-what-is (choice)

Beat 17 consolidation, 2 min. Say: Think about your poem. Expect C; if A/B replay Beats 3+6 evidence (comes/feet did not rhyme; cold/taps were not hard). Follow-up oral: What is one choice you made? Take 3 answers. Bridge: One last challenge — prove it in two lines.

## 21. 2e-b18-exit (writing)

Beat 18 exit evidence/fresh transfer, 2 min. 2E stretch built into task: TWO different tools (vs one in base spine). Frame hint if stuck: The moon ___ (watches, smiles, waits) or like a ___ (lamp, light, eye). Possibles: The moon / watches me. OR The bright moon hangs / like a lamp in the sky. Reward any real choice — human action, comparison, strong break. Per share ask: What choice did the writer make? Final line: You have written a poem. Collect exit lines + Beat 15 poems as evidence.

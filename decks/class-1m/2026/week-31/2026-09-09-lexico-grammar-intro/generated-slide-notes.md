# One Word Out, One Word In — slide notes

## 1. b1-repair-job (image)

SET INDUCTION (~4 min, silent think + whisper). Sayable: Yesterday you picked the missing sentence. Today the sentence is there. One word inside is wrong. You swap that one word. Point at headline, then reveal hint as recall of Bridge/result language.

## 2. b2-go-went (cloze)

MODEL 1 (~5 min, full think-aloud). Cover the sentence, show only go goes to went; everything else stays identical. Sayable: One word out. One word in. Nothing else moves. Corrects spelling-rewrite reflex.

## 3. b3-one-word (cloze)

MODEL 2 (~4 min). Say the broken version aloud first: arrived to school. B sounds longer and better but has a space in it, so it scores zero. Sayable: If your answer has a space, it is two words. One box, one word.

## 4. b4-joining-word (cloze)

GUIDED 1 conjunction (~4 min, silent finger 1/2 + whisper). Redirect verb-tense reflex: did any verb change? No. The verbs are fine. The join is wrong. Whisper cue: twist or reason?

## 5. b5-small-word (cloze)

GUIDED 2 preposition (~4 min). L1 transfer sampai ke makes to tempting. Force the verb test: cover the rest, say arrived at / arrived to aloud. Sayable: the verb before decides the small word after.

## 6. b6-time-verb (cloze)

GUIDED 3 verb (~4 min). Watch for pupils fixing night to nights instead of the verb. Redirect: spelling is fine, grammar time is broken. Follow-up: name the frame, past narrative.

## 7. b7-number-noun (cloze)

GUIDED 4 noun (~4 min). Counter sounds-fine: point at two, then at the noun. These two must agree. Whisper follow-up: show one finger or two, then point at the noun.

## 8. b8-pointing-word (cloze)

GUIDED 5 pronoun (~3 min, fast transfer). Same check as yesterday: pronoun points to the sentence before. If split, ask boy or girl, then which word says that.

## 9. b9-a-an (cloze)

GUIDED 6 article (~3 min). Counter same-meaning: P1P2 tests small words too, one mark each. Transition: six families, let us put them on one card.

## 10. b10-toolkit (passage)

SYNTHESIS (~3 min, reference only, no new teaching, no pending answer). Read aloud once, sayably. State: this card stays up; it is your toolkit, not a question. Keep visible or re-show during the two master challenges.

## 11. b11-master-result (short)

APPLY 1 (~5 min, silent notebook write, sample 2 voices, type one). Redirect verb reflex: verbs already match Last Saturday, check the join. Whisper: name the check first, then the word. Toolkit card stays up.

## 12. b12-master-owner (short)

APPLY 2 (~5 min). Common wrong answer the: sounds fine but loses whose; its keeps the owner. Counter any-small-word: point at the owner, which word carries it.

## 13. b13-exit (prompt)

EXIT EVIDENCE (~3 min, silent finger scan). Record which families get few fingers as the reteach target for worksheet day. Close sayable: one word out, one word in; name the check before you fix.

## 3. hook-vote (choice)

## 4. clue1-teach (passage)

INTRODUCE Check 1 (topic). Say: 'First ask: what are we talking about? Circle the topic words with me.' Do not vote yet.

## 5. clue1-test (reveal)

## 6. clue2-teach (passage)

## 7. clue2-test (choice)

## 8. clue3-teach (passage)

## 9. clue3-test (choice)

## 10. clue4-teach (passage)

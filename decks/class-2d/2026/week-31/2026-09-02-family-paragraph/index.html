ONE TOPIC: MY FAMILY

This is a paragraph.

A paragraph is a group of sentences about one topic.

Today: we build a My Family paragraph with four lines.

MODEL · READ TOGETHER

A My Family paragraph

Four lines, four jobs. Click Next line as we read.

PEOPLEI have one brother and one sister. They are young.
TOGETHERWe eat dinner together and we play games at night.
FEELINGI love my family because they always help me.

OUR WRITING PLAN

Four jobs in four lines

1

START

Name your family.
This is my family.

2

PEOPLE

Who is in it? Use a pronoun.
I have one sister. She is ten.

3

TOGETHER

What do you do? Add with and.
We cook together and we watch TV.

4

FEELING

Why do you love them? Use because.
I love my family because they help me.

Say the four jobs with me: START — PEOPLE — TOGETHER — FEELING.

ERROR CLINIC

Fix the broken line

Say the correct line first. Then reveal.

1  Him is my little brother.

He is my little brother. — He does the action; him receives it.

2  My mother cook dinner every night.

My mother cooks dinner. — she + action → add -s.

3  she is my sister

She is my sister. — big letter, full stop.

RECAP

What did we learn?

1  What is a paragraph?

A group of sentences about one topic.

2  What does because give? What does and add?

because = a reason. and = another idea.

3  What do we use instead of repeating a noun?

A pronoun: he, she, they, we, I.

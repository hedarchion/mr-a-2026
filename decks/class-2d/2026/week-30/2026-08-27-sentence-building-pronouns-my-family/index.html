NEW BUILDING WORDS

I / me / my

Use the word that fits the sentence.

I

___ love my family.

I love my family.
me

My mother helps ___.

My mother helps me.
my

___ brother is eleven.

My brother is eleven.
✓ I starts the action. me comes after the action. my comes before a family thing.
NEW BUILDING WORDS

We / us / our

Two or more people can build one sentence.

We

___ eat dinner together.

We eat dinner together.
us

Mum cooks for ___.

Mum cooks for us.
our

___ favourite food is chicken.

Our favourite food is chicken.
✓ We starts the action. us comes after the action or after “for”. our comes before a thing.

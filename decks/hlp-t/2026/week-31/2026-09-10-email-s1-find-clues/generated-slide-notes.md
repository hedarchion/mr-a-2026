# One Memorised Email · Session 1: Find the Clues — slide notes

## 1. hook-reset (choice)

0:00-2:00. Most pupils pick B (the 08-09 rule) — welcome it, then correct: in EMAILS the reader is the named friend, not the marker. Kill 'no Dear, no From' explicitly: emails KEEP Dear. Latecomers: repeat the one-liner 'We write to a friend today, not the marker.'

## 2. one-email (passage)

2:00-4:00. Teacher circles the friend name after Dear (say it, point, pupils point). Say: 'The name after Dear is the reader. Every email starts here.' Deflect full-reading questions: 'Today we only find the reader. We write in Session 2.' The bracketed boxes are named only, not filled.

## 3. clues-1 (steps)

4:00-10:00, ~1.5 min per clue. Exact script aloud: 'Circle WHAT. WHAT means apa.' / 'Circle WHERE. WHERE means di mana.' (parallel for WHEN/WHO). Elicit Malay first, then fix. Board drills (circle the English clue): What sport do you like? / Where do you play it? / When do you play it? / Who plays with you? One-liner pre-empt: 'WHICH is also apa — it means choose one.'

## 4. clues-2 (steps)

10:00-14:00. Teach explicitly: 'feel after How = FEEL clue, the answer is a feeling.' Redirect answerers: 'Good answer. Now circle the clue word.' Fast round after reveal (oral, no new text): teacher says Malay, class says English — apa→WHAT, di mana→WHERE, bila→WHEN, siapa→WHO, mengapa→WHY, bagaimana→HOW, perasaan→FEEL.

## 5. danger (steps)

14:00-18:00. Exact lines: 'I see AND. I need one more answer.' / 'I see TWO. I need two answers.' Board examples (circle the danger word): I like apples and oranges. / You have two free hours. / Tell me about your day. / Include your phone number. Distinguish once: TELL the danger word = an order inside the question; the TELL card = which email to write. Same word, two jobs.

## 6. drill-a (reveal)

18:00-19:00. Pupils circle on the worksheet, then hold up fingers for the box count. Fast whole-class check.

## 7. drill-b (reveal)

19:00-20:00. Common miss: AND skipped, answer '1' — replay 'I see AND. I need one more answer.' WHICH belongs to the WHAT family (choose one).

## 8. drill-c (reveal)

20:00-21:00. 'favourite' is not one of the seven circle clues — it is a TELL-card word for later (Beat 7); today circle TWO and TELL. TELL here is an order, not yet the green card.

## 9. cards (steps)

22:00-26:00, 1 min per prompt. Four oral prompts (teacher reads, pupils hold up colour or say card): 1. Which sport should I choose? (HELP, blue — SHOULD) 2. Tell me about your favourite game. (TELL, green — tell and favourite; correct the WHAT-misfire: 'WHAT is the clue, but tell sets the job.') 3. I invite you to my party. (COME, orange — invite is stronger than tell) 4. I am sorry I broke your pen. (SORRY, red). Exact line: 'I see SHOULD. Choose the blue HELP card.' Keep colours visible; pointing is fine if no cards in hand. did/went are also TELL triggers (card row, toolkit, worksheet item 7).

## 10. toolkit (passage)

26:00-27:00. Read once straight through if needed, then point — do not teach from it. Keep this slide open (navigate back to it) during the guided model and the exit. No hints or reveals on a reference.

## 11. sport-model-a (reveal)

27:00-28:00. Routine part 1 (pupils repeat and do): 'Circle WHAT — here WHICH, also apa. I see SHOULD. Choose the blue HELP card.'

## 12. sport-model-b (reveal)

28:00-29:00. Routine part 2: 'Circle WHERE. Circle WHO. Three questions — three boxes.' Stop sentence-writing: 'Boxes today. Sentences in Session 2.'

## 13. exit (image)

29:00-30:00. Independent evidence: pupils complete READER + CARD + CIRCLES + BOXES on worksheet Part 4 alone (slates/paper if sheets already used). Dictate the frame: READER / CARD (circle HELP·TELL·COME·SORRY) / CIRCLES / BOXES. Collect before revealing the key. Marking offline per the key.

## 14. exit-key (reveal)

Reveal only after every sheet is collected. Pass = reader (friend) + HELP + at least 3 circles (WHICH/WHERE/WHO) + boxes 3. Fail patterns → Session 2 re-teach: wrong card (SHOULD→HELP), count under 3 (every question needs its box), reader = marker (Dear rule). Record aggregate only.

## Exit-pass rule (item 8 + sheet)

The pupil passes Session 1 iff: reader = friend, card = HELP, at least 3 clue words circled (WHICH/WHERE/WHO), box count = 3. Items 1–7 are guided practice; item 8 is the independent evidence and must be completed after collection of no help.

## Fail patterns → Session 2 re-teach

- Wrong card (e.g. TELL for item 8): re-teach SHOULD → blue HELP.
- Box count under 3 on item 8: every question word needs its own box.
- Reader = marker: re-teach the Dear rule (emails KEEP Dear; reader is the name after it).
- Missing AND in item 2: replay "I see AND. I need one more answer."

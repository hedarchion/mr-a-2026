# See the Clues and Count the Answers · Session 1 — slide notes

## 1. hook-find (image)

0-5 hook. Chant find, circle, count twice; red pencil up on circle. Boxes only today; keep the three verbs on the wall all session.

## 2. reader-marker (choice)

Circle ESSAY on paper. Reader is the marker. Park the Q59 trap: the teacher is IN that story but the reader is still the marker; prove it later.

## 3. no-dear-from (choice)

Worksheet A item 4. All 60 tasks in this system are essays, so never add letter parts.

## 4. clues-a (steps)

Chorus each twice. Red pencil underlines the clue word in every later note. BM meanings are small support only.

## 5. clues-b (steps)

Repair HOW vs WHY: how to keep fit names the way; benefits name the reason. All six labels are reused on every later beat.

## 6. q1-model (choice)

Think aloud using only the six clue words. Cover WHERE and ask: WHEN alone is still an answer, so two boxes. Blue pencil TIME, red pencil PLACE. Four fingers.

## 7. split-rule (reveal)

Repair counting OR as two: OR means one action done well. Repair merging comma lists into one blob. The fourth split, what you saw and bought, stays on the worksheet to avoid overload.

## 8. q27-count (choice)

Underline the doing-words in red. Contrast with OR: no choice here, all three count. Bridge: three answers, but what kind of writing? That is the job card.

## 9. job-cards (steps)

Hold-up check, six prompts: a trip you enjoyed PAST; benefits of reading FACT; your future creation PLAN; how to keep fit ADVICE; took part in a badminton match PAST; Eat balanced food ADVICE. Repair benefits vs advice: benefits TELLS a fact, advice TELLS what to do. Keep the four cards on the wall.

## 10. synthesis-toolkit (compare)

Synthesis reference before application: all six clues, three split rules, four jobs and the reader rule together. No new teaching and no Q17 or Q59 answers here. Optional 60-second mask-and-recall game.

## 11. race-flag (image)

Timer about three minutes per race. Pairs circle, split and count only. Synthesis reference stays open. Hardest count first.

## 12. q17-try (passage)

Dataset maximum. Expect eight boxes: three badminton facts, three table-tennis facts, choice plus reason. Repair merging popularity across games, and dropping the reason.

## 13. q17-check (choice)

Reveal the eight-way split. Choice WITHOUT reason is half the last two marks: WHY always needs its own box.

## 14. q59-reader-trap (choice)

Four boxes plus NO to reader-is-teacher. No Dear and no you: her name goes in a WHO box. SHE sentence bricks belong to Session 2, not now. Copy debating team accurately.

## 15. exit-check (image)

Reference closed. Set A trip: WHEN+WHERE two, SAW+BOUGHT two, felt one, total five, job PAST. Set B hands: OR chooses one, balanced food one, sleep one, total three, job ADVICE. Four minutes strict.

## 16. exit-key (reveal)

Mark five per set: reader one, job one, splits and counts, clue labels. Mastery eight of ten including one exact compound count. Below threshold gets a five-minute re-sort in Session 2; keep that list private.
